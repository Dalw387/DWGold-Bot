# DW Gold Trading Glossary

## Account Balance
The amount in the account after closed trades, deposits, and withdrawals.

## Ask Price
The price used when buying.

## Bid Price
The price used when selling.

## Break Even
When a trade is protected around the entry price so it should not lose if price returns.

## Broker
A company that provides access to trading markets and platforms.

## Candle Body
The thick part of the candle showing the open and close price.

## Equity
The live account value including open trades.

## Free Margin
Money available after margin is being used by open trades.

## Margin Call
A warning or action when the account does not have enough margin to safely keep trades open.

## Order
An instruction placed on the platform, such as opening or closing a trade.

## Pending Order
An order set to trigger later if price reaches a chosen level.

## Spread
The gap between the buy price and sell price.

## Volatility
How much and how quickly price moves.

## Wick
The thin line on a candle showing the high or low reached during that candle.
