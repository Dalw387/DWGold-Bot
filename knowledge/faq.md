# DW Gold Trading FAQ

## Do you give signals?
DW Gold Trading provides education only. We do not provide trading signals, financial advice, investment advice, or market predictions.

## Can you tell me when to buy or sell gold?
DW Gold Trading provides education only. We do not provide buy or sell recommendations, trading signals, financial advice, investment advice, or market predictions.

## Can you predict where gold is going today?
DW Gold Trading provides education only. We do not provide trading signals, financial advice, investment advice, or market predictions.

## Can I learn on a demo account first?
Yes. A demo account is a sensible way to practise using MT5, placing trades, setting stop loss and take profit, and understanding lot size without risking real money.

## Is trading risky?
Yes. Trading is risky and you can lose money. Beginners should focus on education, risk management, demo practice, and only trade money they can afford to lose.

## What should I learn first?
Start with stop loss, take profit, lot size, spread, support, resistance, risk management, MT5 basics, and demo practice.
