# DW Gold Trading Abbreviations

## SL
SL means Stop Loss. It is the safety level where a trade closes if price moves against it.

## TP
TP means Take Profit. It is the target level where a trade closes in profit.

## RR
RR means Risk to Reward. It compares how much is being risked against how much could potentially be made.

## BE
BE means Break Even. It usually means the stop loss has been moved to the entry price.

## FVG
FVG means Fair Value Gap. It is an imbalance area on the chart caused by fast price movement.

## BOS
BOS means Break of Structure. It means price has broken an important previous high or low.

## CHOCH
CHOCH means Change of Character. It can suggest market behaviour may be changing, but it is not guaranteed.

## EMA
EMA means Exponential Moving Average. It is a moving average that reacts faster to recent price.

## SMA
SMA means Simple Moving Average. It is the average price over a chosen number of candles.

## ATR
ATR means Average True Range. It shows how much price is moving on average.

## RSI
RSI means Relative Strength Index. It is a momentum indicator often used to understand overbought or oversold conditions.

## MACD
MACD means Moving Average Convergence Divergence. It is a momentum indicator used by some traders to understand trend strength and changes.

## CPI
CPI means Consumer Price Index. It is inflation data and can affect gold because inflation and interest-rate expectations matter to markets.

## NFP
NFP means Non-Farm Payrolls. It is a major US jobs report that can cause strong movement in gold.

## FOMC
FOMC means Federal Open Market Committee. It is connected to US interest-rate decisions, which can strongly affect gold.

## DXY
DXY is the US Dollar Index. Gold often reacts to dollar strength or weakness, but the relationship is not guaranteed.

## XAUUSD
XAUUSD is the trading symbol for gold priced against the US dollar.

## ATH
ATH means All-Time High. It is the highest price ever reached.

## ATL
ATL means All-Time Low. It is the lowest price ever reached.
